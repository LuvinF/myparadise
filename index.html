To My Dearest Queen

Five Months of Us

A small eternity, written in the stars

♡   ✧   ♡

My fairest love, if time were but a quill,
And every star a word upon its page,
I'd write of thee through centuries still,
And make eternity our marriage stage.

Five moons have passed since fate, with gentle hand,
Did weave our souls beneath one heaven's glow;
Yet still my heart, as though enchanted, stands,
In wondrous awe of all thy love doth show.

Thou art my dawn when all the skies are grey,
My sweetest dream that waking cannot sever;
And should the stars themselves fade away,
My love for thee shall know no end, nor ever.

For in this royal paradise of mine,
No crown, no throne, no treasure could compare;
For all the kingdoms underneath the sky,
Are but poor shadows next to thee, my fairest rare.

“ In the Royal Paradise of my heart, thou art not merely my queen; thou art the kingdom, the crown, and every star that makes my universe divine. ”
✧   ❦   ✧

Happy Five-Monthversary, my love.

Five months may seem like a little chapter in eternity, but with thee, every second has become a lifetime worth cherishing. If I could choose again, across a thousand lifetimes and a million stars, my heart would still find its way to thee.

Forever thine, through every lifetime. ❤️
